Streaming FAQ

Everything you need to know about broadcasting on TalkTap.

How can I become a Verified Streamer?
Users can apply for the "Verified Creator" badge in the app settings by providing a valid ID and completing a short orientation on our Community Guidelines.
What makes TalkTap different from dating apps?
Unlike dating apps, TalkTap is built for broadcasting talent (singing, podcasting, education). We strictly prohibit 1-on-1 dating requests and focus on public community interactions.
How do I earn rewards from live streaming?
Creators can receive Virtual Gifts from their fans during live sessions. These gifts can be converted into real earnings based on our Creator Payment Policy.
What happens if someone violates rules in my stream?
You can instantly ban them from your room. Our AI and human moderators also receive an alert to review the user's account for a permanent platform-wide ban.
Are there age restrictions for broadcasters?
Yes, all broadcasters must be at least 18 years old. We use age-verification technology to ensure a safe and mature environment for professional creators.
