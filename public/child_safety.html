Child Safety Standards

TalkTap is strictly a platform for adults (18+). We are deeply committed to protecting minors and maintain a strict zero-tolerance policy against any form of child endangerment or exploitation.

Our Protective Measures

We employ advanced technology and strict community guidelines to ensure our platform remains safe and inaccessible to minors.

Strict Age Restrictions

TalkTap is strictly restricted to users aged 18 and older. We use age-gating mechanisms during registration to prevent underage users from accessing the platform.

Proactive AI Monitoring

Our intelligent AI systems constantly scan profiles, audio streams, and text chats to detect and flag any suspicious activity related to minors.

Law Enforcement Cooperation

We work closely with organizations like NCMEC and local law enforcement agencies to immediately report and investigate any potential threats to children.

Zero-Tolerance Child Endangerment Policy

In compliance with global child protection laws (including COPPA), TalkTap immediately terminates accounts and reports violators to the authorities. We strictly prohibit the following:

  • Users under the age of 18
  • Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM)
  • Grooming or Predatory Behavior
  • Sharing Minors' Personal Information
  • Endangering or Exploiting Minors
  • Falsifying Age during Registration

Child Safety FAQ

Common questions regarding age restrictions and reporting mechanisms on TalkTap.

What is the minimum age to use TalkTap?
You must be at least 18 years old to create an account, broadcast, or interact on TalkTap. We do not allow teenagers or children on our platform.
How does TalkTap verify user age?
During onboarding, users must confirm they are over 18. Additionally, our moderation team and AI systems monitor accounts. If an account is suspected to belong to a minor, it will be immediately suspended pending verification.
How do I report a child safety concern?
You can report any suspicious activity using the in-app "Report" button on a user's profile or live stream. For urgent child safety issues, you can directly email our Trust & Safety team at safety@talktap.com. We prioritize these reports immediately.
Do you report violators to the police?
Yes, absolutely. We have a legal and moral obligation to report any instances involving the exploitation of minors directly to organizations like the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and relevant local law enforcement.
