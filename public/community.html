Broadcaster Guidelines

TalkTap is built for creators, streamers, and talent discovery. These guidelines ensure a professional environment for both broadcasters and viewers.

Professionalism

Treat your stream as a professional stage. Engagement should be respectful, creative, and talent-focused.

Broadcaster Security

Maintain clear boundaries between creators and fans. Never solicit or share private personal information.

Creative Integrity

Be original. Showcase your own skills, music, and art. Impersonation and piracy are strictly prohibited.

Streaming Standards

Creator Standards

  • Talent Showcase

    Focus your live sessions on singing, educational content, gaming, podcasts, or community discussions.

  • Moderation Power

    Use your host controls to keep your chat clean. Ban disruptive viewers who deviate from professional behavior.

  • Transparent Rewards

    Encourage support through virtual gifts based on your talent and performance quality.

  • Quality Audio/Video

    Ensure your streams meet our platform’s quality standards for a better user experience.

Strictly Prohibited

  • Dating & Hookup Content

    TalkTap is NOT a dating app. Seeking romantic partners or using streams for hookups is a banable offense.

  • Provocative Attire/Behavior

    Adult content, explicit language, or provocative behavior is strictly banned to protect our community standards.

  • Solicitation & Scams

    Do not use streams to sell illegal products, promote gambling, or solicit money outside of the gifting system.

  • Hate Speech & Toxicity

    Discrimination against any group or individual will result in an immediate and permanent platform ban.

Policy Enforcement

Our AI-moderation and human safety teams review streams 24/7. Violations of the Broadcaster Guidelines lead to immediate termination of the live stream and permanent account suspension without warning.
